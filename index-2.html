For the Heelers we love. And the ones still waiting.

No Heeler Left Behind helps Australian Cattle Dogs, Heelers and mixes in U.S. municipal and county shelters get seen by the networkers, adopters, fosters and rescues who can save them.

We are not a rescue. We exist as one tool to help get these Heelers seen and to make sharing them easier and more effective.

We’re currently in development.
